# Acme Cloud Solutions — Product FAQ

## What plans do you offer?

We offer three plans:

- **Starter** — $29/month: 5 users, 10 GB storage, community support.
- **Pro** — $99/month: 25 users, 100 GB storage, 24/7 priority support,
  API access, SSO.
- **Enterprise** — custom pricing: unlimited users, dedicated infrastructure,
  99.99% uptime SLA, dedicated account manager.

Annual billing gives a 20% discount on all plans.

## Do you have an API?

Yes. The REST API is available on Pro and Enterprise plans. Rate limits are
1,000 requests/minute on Pro and custom limits on Enterprise. API keys are
managed from Settings → Developer. WebSocket streaming endpoints are
available for real-time events.

## How do I migrate my data from another provider?

The built-in migration assistant supports imports from CSV, PostgreSQL,
MySQL, and MongoDB. Enterprise customers get free white-glove migration
performed by our solutions team. A typical migration of 100 GB completes in
under 4 hours.

## What integrations are supported?

Native integrations include Slack, HubSpot, Salesforce, Zapier, and
Microsoft Teams. Webhooks can push events to any HTTPS endpoint. The Zapier
integration unlocks 5,000+ additional apps.

## How do I cancel my subscription?

You can cancel anytime from Billing → Manage Subscription. Your plan remains
active until the end of the current billing period. See the Refund Policy
for refund eligibility.
