# Acme Cloud Solutions — Company Policies

## Refund Policy

Customers may request a full refund within 30 days of purchase for any
subscription plan. After 30 days, refunds are prorated based on the unused
portion of the billing period. Refunds are processed within 5–7 business days
to the original payment method. Enterprise contracts follow the refund terms
specified in their Master Service Agreement. To request a refund, email
billing@acmecloud.example or open a ticket from the customer portal.

## Vacation / Paid Time Off Policy

Full-time employees accrue 1.5 days of paid time off (PTO) per month,
totaling 18 days per year. Up to 5 unused PTO days may be carried over into
the next calendar year. PTO requests must be submitted through the HR portal
at least 2 weeks in advance for leaves longer than 3 days. In addition to
PTO, the company observes 10 public holidays per year.

## Support Hours and SLAs

Standard support is available Monday through Friday, 9:00 AM to 6:00 PM IST,
via email and chat. Priority support (Pro and Enterprise plans) is available
24/7 with the following response-time SLAs:

- Critical (production down): 1 hour
- High (major feature impaired): 4 hours
- Normal (general questions): 1 business day

## Security and Data Privacy

All customer data is encrypted at rest (AES-256) and in transit (TLS 1.3).
Acme Cloud Solutions is SOC 2 Type II certified and GDPR compliant. Customer
data is never used to train machine-learning models without explicit written
consent. Data deletion requests are honored within 30 days.
