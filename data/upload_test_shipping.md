# Acme Cloud Solutions — Shipping & Hardware Policy

## Hardware Shipping

Customers on the Enterprise plan who order the Acme Edge Appliance receive
free express shipping worldwide. Standard delivery takes 6 business days
within India and 10 business days internationally. Each appliance ships with
a 3-year hardware warranty and a prepaid return label.

## Returns

Hardware may be returned within 45 days in original packaging for a full
refund. Opened appliances are subject to a 15% restocking fee.
